Privacy and analytics

Visitor information

This website is an academic profile. It does not require account registration and collects only the information described below.

Analytics

What is measured

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used. It records aggregate information such as page views, approximate (city/region-level) location, referral source, device and browser type, and a small number of interaction events: opening a presentation PDF, clicking an outbound link, and submitting the contact form. The message content is not sent to Google Analytics.

IP addresses are anonymized, and the analytics are configured for aggregate reporting. Aggregate website analytics cannot reliably identify a visitor's name, email address, or private identity.

Cookies and opting out

Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish returning visits. You can block analytics by using your browser's tracking-protection settings, an ad/tracker blocker, or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site remains fully functional with analytics blocked.

Messages and external links

Contact-form submissions are processed by FormSubmit and delivered by email to waelkhateeb.lb@gmail.com and eaus559@eau.ac.ae. Do not include sensitive or confidential information in the form.

Links to journal DOI pages, Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn, and other external sites are governed by the privacy practices of their respective providers.
