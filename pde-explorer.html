    For teaching: heat and transport use exact Gaussian solutions on an idealized unbounded plane; the vibrating membrane uses a damped standing-wave mode on a square; the wing uses steady, incompressible, inviscid potential flow around a Joukowski airfoil, not a turbulence/viscous CFD solver. Plotted surface height represents the scalar solution for the first three models. On the wing, the 3D shape and streamlines represent a spanwise extension of a two-dimensional flow model.
